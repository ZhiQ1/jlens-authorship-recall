Teacher-forced preference, not generation.

**What "no exclusion" means.** The probe is fitted on true-title residuals; a paper whose false-title donor is a training-fold paper can reuse that donor's true title. The published versions drop such papers (V5x0, V0) or such false titles (Vpd). Here every paper and every false title is kept, so these numbers **include that leakage** and are a sensitivity check, not a replacement of the published values. Without exclusion the per-donor version (Vpd_noexcl) and the five-title version (V5x0_noexcl) are the same quantity (true minus the mean of all five false titles); both were computed through their own code path and agree.
