<h2>Does the title effect survive the obvious objections?</h2>
<div class="row" style="margin-top:10px">
 <div class="col box t"><p class="a" style="font-size:24px">Source-author overlap</p>
 199/1,000 wrong titles come from a paper whose author is in the false pool. Excluding them: +0.026 → +0.028.</div>
 <div class="col box t"><p class="a" style="font-size:24px">Candidate weighting</p>
 Author-, unique-token- and non-collision weights: pooled +0.026 to +0.032; all six A−B CIs include 0.</div>
 <div class="col box t"><p class="a" style="font-size:24px">Low citations</p>
 0–2 citations (n = 6): L56 +0.22, 5/6 positive, but 0/6 beyond its own 20-title tail (≤ 0.05).</div>
