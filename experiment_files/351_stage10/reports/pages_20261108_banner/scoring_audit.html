0. Conventions every Stage 10–12 per-token score shares

  • Context. Each author is scored alone: PROMPT + " " + author, teacher-forced. Token j is scored at the position just before it (S10:118–136, 563–564).
  • Authors. Every author in the label file is kept. The authors column is split on ";" (S10:118). The cohort has 33,811 author contexts and 149,999 name tokens.
  • Paper value. For each paper, the paper value is the token-weighted mean over all name tokens of all authors of nlr = log10(1/rank) (S10:651–656, mean_nlr). The per-author mean author_mean_nlr (S10:657) and role-specific means (S10:659–662) are secondary.
  • Deviation from the frozen protocol. protocol.json names the primary metric as "sum of 1/rank" (S10:165). Every analysis instead uses the mean log10(1/rank). This choice of metric does not restrict which tokens are scored, but it should be stated in the methods.
