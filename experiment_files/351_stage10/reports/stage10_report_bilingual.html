大模型在哪一层"记住"论文作者：逐层读出实验报告 At which layer does Qwen3-32B hold a paper's authors? A layer-wise readout study

9,108 papers33,811 author contexts149,999 name tokens7 title conditions5 readouts × 6 layers + output

§1摘要 Summary

本实验用 9,108 篇论文，逐层读出 Qwen3-32B 内部对作者名字的预测，看模型在哪一层开始"拿着"某篇论文的作者。我们比较了两组论文：一组是模型写不出作者、但在多选题里能认出正确作者名单的"目标组"（target），另一组是既写不出也认不出的"对照组"（control）。为了区分"认识这篇论文"和"名字本身好猜"，每篇论文还换成 5 个匹配好的假标题和删掉标题再测一遍。

Using 9,108 papers, we read out Qwen3-32B's internal predictions of author-name tokens layer by layer to find where the model starts to hold a specific paper's authors. We compare a target group (the model cannot write the authors but recognises the true author list in a multiple-choice test) with a control group (neither writes nor recognises them). To separate knowing the paper from names that are simply easy to guess, every paper is also scored with 5 matched false titles and with the title deleted.

主要结论：针对具体论文的作者信息只在最后几层才读得出来。L24 到 L48 层，真标题相对假标题的优势（下称"标题特异信号"）几乎为 0；从 L50 前后开始逐层积累，L57–L61 上升最快，L61–L62 和最终输出最强。目标组比对照组多出的部分也集中在 L62/L63（在引用数分档内比较，+0.21 到 +0.23，95% 置信区间都在 0 以上），而且几乎全部是标题特异的，不能用名字好猜来解释。中间层没有看到"提前拿着作者"的迹象。

Main result: paper-specific author information becomes readable only in the last layers. From L24 to L48 the advantage of the true title over false titles (the title-specific signal) is close to zero; it builds up layer by layer from about L50, rises fastest over L57–L61, and is strongest at L61–L62 and the model output. The extra signal of the target group over the control group is likewise concentrated at L62/L63 (within citation bins, +0.21 to +0.23, all 95% CIs above zero) and is almost entirely title-specific, so it is not explained by easy-to-guess names. No sign of the authors being held earlier in the middle layers.

补充分析（不需要新的 GPU 计算）进一步表明：标题特异信号在名字里的位置取决于名字格式，首字母开头时几乎都在后续 token（含姓）上（目标−对照 +0.253，首字母本身 +0.048），完整名开头时名和后续 token 各带一份相当的信号（+0.174 与 +0.202）；引用数越高，标题特异信号越强（Spearman ρ = 0.45）；三种 Jacobian lens 在中间层只比 logit lens 多读出很少的论文信息（+0.01 到 +0.03），而且这部分信息不偏向目标组。

Follow-up analyses (no new GPU runs) show further that where the title-specific signal sits within a name depends on its format: for initial-first names it is almost all on the later tokens, which include the surname (target − control +0.253 versus +0.048 on the initial), while for given-name-first names the given name and the later tokens carry comparable shares (+0.174 and +0.202); that it grows with citation count (Spearman ρ = 0.45); and that the three Jacobian lenses read only slightly more paper information than the logit lens in middle layers (+0.01 to +0.03), information that does not favour the target group.

干预实验（Stage 11，§6.11）进一步确定了时间：把真标题运行中"标题之后的 token"的表示替换进假标题运行，L48 已恢复 83%，L52 起恢复 100%；从 L52 起屏蔽对标题的注意力已没有影响。也就是说，论文信息在 L24–L50 就被读取并搬到标题之后的位置，只是要到 L56–62 才变成 lens 能读出的形式。

Interventions (Stage 11, §6.11) fix the timing: copying the hidden states of the tokens after the title from the true-title run into the false-title run recovers 83% at L48 and 100% from L52, and blocking attention to the title from L52 on has no effect. The paper information is read and moved into the post-title positions in L24–L50; it only becomes lens-readable in L56–62.

§2研究问题 Question

此前的标注实验发现，Qwen3-32B 对大约一半的论文"写不出作者、但认得出作者名单"。本阶段要回答的是：这种"认得"在网络内部是什么时候形成的？如果模型在中间层就已经把作者信息算出来、只是没能输出，那么用 lens 读中间层时，目标组应该比对照组更容易读出正确的名字；如果信息只在最后几层才组装出来，差异就只会出现在输出附近。

An earlier labelling run found that for roughly half of the papers Qwen3-32B cannot write the authors yet recognises the true author list. This stage asks when that recognition forms inside the network. If the model already computes the author information in middle layers and only fails to emit it, a lens applied to those layers should read the correct name more easily for target papers than for control papers. If the information is assembled only in the last layers, the difference should appear only near the output.

§3数据 Data

论文集合来自 2026-10-15 的 Qwen3-32B 标注包（paper_labels_with_papers.csv），共 9,108 篇，覆盖 8 个领域（医学 1,230、生物 1,179、计算机 1,174、心理学 1,154、化学 1,134、数学 1,088、物理 1,082、材料 1,067）。发表年份 1817–2020（中位数 2006），引用数 0–63,901（中位数 50.5），按引用数分成 13 档（0-2, 3-4, 5-8 … 2049-4096, 4097+）。每篇论文平均 3.7 位作者（1–20 位）。

The paper set is the 2026-10-15 Qwen3-32B label package (paper_labels_with_papers.csv): 9,108 papers across 8 fields (medicine 1,230, biology 1,179, computer science 1,174, psychology 1,154, chemistry 1,134, mathematics 1,088, physics 1,082, materials science 1,067). Publication years span 1817–2020 (median 2006), citations 0–63,901 (median 50.5), grouped into 13 citation bins (0-2, 3-4, 5-8 … 2049-4096, 4097+). Papers have 3.7 authors on average (1–20).

分组沿用标注包的 group_tight：先让模型开放回答"这篇论文的作者是谁"，再给 4 个候选作者名单（真名单 + 同领域、相近年份和引用档的 3 个真实团队）按 4 种顺序做多选。能写出作者的是 guard（R2）；写不出、但 4 次全对且真选项平均概率 ≥ 0.5 的是 target；写不出且最多对 1 次的是 control；其余为 excluded。

Groups follow the package's group_tight label. The model first answers openly "who wrote this paper", then picks among 4 candidate author lists (the true list plus 3 real teams from papers of the same field, similar year and citation bin) in 4 orders. Papers whose authors it can write are guard (R2); papers it cannot write but gets right in all 4 orders with mean true-option probability ≥ 0.5 are target; papers it cannot write and gets right at most once are control; the rest are excluded.

§4方法 Method

4.1 输入与打分 Input and scoring

模型为 Qwen/Qwen3-32B（revision 9216db57），bf16，单张 A100-80GB。提示词为 Who are the authors of the paper titled "{title}" ({year})?\nAuthors:，后面接一位作者的名字。用教师强制（teacher forcing）：在每个名字 token 之前的位置，看模型给正确 token 排第几名。

The model is Qwen/Qwen3-32B (revision 9216db57) in bf16 on one A100-80GB. The prompt is Who are the authors of the paper titled "{title}" ({year})?\nAuthors: followed by one author's name. With teacher forcing, at the position before each name token we record the rank of the correct token.

主指标是 log10(1/名次)：排第 1 为 0，排第 10 为 −1，词表最差约 −5.18。每篇论文对所有名字 token 取平均，再在组内对论文平均；组间比较只用平均值，不用总和（各组作者数不同）。

The main metric is log10(1/rank): rank 1 gives 0, rank 10 gives −1, the worst possible is about −5.18. It is averaged over a paper's name tokens and then over papers within a group; groups are compared by means only, never sums, because author counts differ between groups.

4.2 读出方式与层 Readouts and layers

在第 24、32、40、48、56、62 层的残差流上用 4 种方式读出，另加模型真实输出（L63）：

The residual stream at layers 24, 32, 40, 48, 56 and 62 is read out in four ways, plus the model's actual output (L63):

  • logit lens：直接接最终归一化和输出层。final norm + unembedding applied directly.
  • jlens_public：公开的 Jacobian lens（Neuronpedia，557 条 wikitext 提示词拟合，覆盖全部层）。public Jacobian lens from Neuronpedia, fitted on 557 wikitext prompts, all layers.
  • jlens_self100：自己拟合的 Jacobian lens（100 条提示词）。our own Jacobian lens fitted on 100 prompts.
  • jlens_fitA：Stage 9 修复后的 Fit A lens（100 条提示词）。the repaired Stage 9 Fit A lens, 100 prompts.
  • final：模型真实下一个 token 的输出（L63）。the model's real next-token output (L63).

Jacobian lens 先把第 l 层的残差用该层的 Jacobian 矩阵变换到最后一层的空间，再接输出层，比 logit lens 更能读出中间层的信息。

A Jacobian lens first transports the layer-l residual into the last layer's space with that layer's Jacobian matrix and then applies the unembedding, which reads middle-layer information better than the logit lens.

4.3 标题条件 Title conditions

每篇论文测 7 种提示：真标题（true）；5 个假标题（false_0–false_4，借用同领域其他论文的标题，年份保持不变）；删掉标题（delete：Who are the authors of the paper ({year})?）。假标题按年份、作者数、标题长度和引用档的差距做最小代价匹配，排除共享姓氏或标题过于相似的论文。匹配分 4 级：strict 35,898 个、medium 1,638 个、loose 2,565 个、nearest 5,439 个。

Each paper is scored under 7 prompts: the true title; 5 false titles (false_0–false_4, titles of other papers in the same field, year kept); and the title deleted (Who are the authors of the paper ({year})?). False titles are matched by minimum-cost assignment on year, author count, title length and citation-bin distance, excluding donors with a shared surname or a too-similar title. Matches fall into 4 tiers: strict 35,898, medium 1,638, loose 2,565, nearest 5,439.

由此得到三种对比（同一组 token，配对比较）：标题特异 = 真标题 − 5 个假标题的平均，衡量"认识这篇论文"；总效应 = 真标题 − 删掉标题；主题效应 = 假标题平均 − 删掉标题，衡量任何相近标题都能带来的帮助。

This gives three paired contrasts on the same tokens: title-specific = true − mean of the 5 false titles, measuring knowledge of this particular paper; total = true − deleted; topic = mean false − deleted, the help any similar title gives.

4.4 统计 Statistics

目标组与对照组只在同一引用档内比较（两组都至少 30 篇的档），按 nt·nc/(nt+nc) 加权合并，因为引用数和分组高度相关（0-2 档目标组占 25%，4097+ 档占 99%）。置信区间用档内、组内重抽论文的 bootstrap（2,000 次）。敏感性分析去掉 0-4 两个低引用档（这两档的"认出"接近随机偏好的 25%）。

Target and control are compared only within citation bins (bins with at least 30 papers in both groups), pooled with weights nt·nc/(nt+nc), because citations and group are strongly confounded (target share 25% in bin 0-2, 99% in 4097+). Confidence intervals come from a bootstrap of papers within bin and group (2,000 draws). A sensitivity analysis drops the two lowest bins 0-4, where recognition is close to the 25% rate of a fixed preference.

§5计算与质量控制 Computation and checks

论文分成 72 个 shard（每个 128 篇）。主轮共 7 个条件 × 72 个 shard × 25 组"读出 × 层"，输出 12,600 个文件，全部完成。计算在 Explorer 集群的 A100-80GB 上进行，主轮实际约 7 GPU 小时，最多 4 张卡并行；每个 shard 约 46 秒。所有输入（论文集合、token 表、提示词、lens 文件）在运行前冻结并做 sha256 校验。

Papers are split into 72 shards of 128. The main round is 7 conditions × 72 shards × 25 readout-layer pairs, 12,600 output files, all complete. It ran on A100-80GB GPUs of the Explorer cluster, about 7 GPU-hours with up to 4 GPUs in parallel, about 46 s per shard. All inputs (paper set, token table, prompts, lens files) were frozen and sha256-checked before the run.

一致性检查（parity）。快速路径（整段一次前向）和参考路径（每个前缀单独前向）在 12 个样本、52 个位置上比较。两者数学上等价，差异只来自 bf16 累加顺序：最大 |Δlogit| 为 0.16–0.37，最大差出现在不经过 lens 的最终输出（0.367），排名前 41 位在所有位置完全一致。原定容差 0.25，看到结果后放宽到 0.5，这是事后调整，已记录在分析计划中。

Parity check. The fast path (one forward pass over the sequence) was compared with a reference (a separate forward pass per prefix) on 12 sampled contexts and 52 positions. The two are mathematically identical; differences come only from bf16 accumulation order. Max |Δlogit| was 0.16–0.37, the largest on the final output, which involves no lens code (0.367), and ranks 1–41 matched exactly at every position. The pre-set tolerance of 0.25 was raised to 0.5 after seeing these results; this post-hoc change is recorded in the analysis plan.

与 Stage 6 交叉核对。972 个重叠 token 的 token id 100% 一致，对数概率差的中位数为 3×10−6 到 7×10−5，排名完全相同的比例为 77–95%，其余差异同样来自 bf16 舍入。

Cross-check with Stage 6. On 972 overlapping tokens the token ids match 100%, the median log-probability difference is 3×10−6 to 7×10−5, and 77–95% of ranks are identical; the remaining differences are again bf16 rounding.

6.3 标题特异信号 Title-specific signal

把真标题和 5 个假标题对比后，名字本身好不好猜的影响就被抵消了。结果很清楚：L24–L48 层，两组的标题特异信号都不超过 0.05；L56 开始上升（+0.10 到 +0.18）；L62 达到最大，目标组 +0.48 到 +0.58，对照组 +0.20 到 +0.25。对照组也有一定的标题特异信号，说明"认不出名单"的论文模型也并非完全不认识。

Contrasting the true title with 5 false titles cancels out how guessable a name is. The picture is clear: at L24–L48 the title-specific signal is at most 0.05 in both groups; it rises at L56 (+0.10 to +0.18) and peaks at L62, +0.48 to +0.58 for target and +0.20 to +0.25 for control. The control group also shows some title-specific signal, so papers whose author list the model fails to pick are not entirely unknown to it.

6.5 阴性对照与 token 角色 Negative control and token roles

标点 token（名字里的点和空格）不应该依赖论文标题。它们的标题特异信号在所有层都接近 0（最大 0.014），说明对比方法本身不会凭空产生信号。信号主要落在姓的第一个 token 上（L62 为 +0.80 到 +0.88），名的第一个 token 次之，首字母最少。

Punctuation tokens (the dots and spaces in names) should not depend on the title. Their title-specific signal is near zero at every layer (at most 0.014), so the contrast does not create signal by itself. The signal sits mostly on the first surname token (+0.80 to +0.88 at L62), less on the first given-name token, and least on initials.

6.6 与多选题成绩的等级关系 Graded relation with the multiple-choice score

不分组、直接按多选题答对次数（0–4）看最终输出（在引用档内中心化）：答对 0 次 −0.137、1 次 −0.122、2 次 −0.047、3 次 −0.018、4 次 +0.076，单调上升，档内 Spearman ρ = 0.27（p < 0.001）。多选题"认得"越稳，输出时越容易说出作者。

Without grouping, by the number of correct multiple-choice orders (0–4), the final output (centred within citation bin) rises monotonically: −0.137, −0.122, −0.047, −0.018, +0.076, with a within-bin Spearman ρ of 0.27 (p < 0.001). The more consistently the model recognises the author list, the more easily it produces the authors at the output.

6.7 第一个 token 与后续 token First token vs later tokens

每位作者的名字被切成若干 token（平均 4.4 个）。这里把第 1 个 token 和后续 token 分开统计。要注意，在教师强制下，后续 token 前面已经给出了正确的前缀（例如名字的前半部分），所以它们本来就更容易猜，这是预期内的，不能直接解读成"模型更认识姓"。

Each author name is split into several tokens (4.4 on average). Here the first token is analysed separately from the later tokens. Under teacher forcing the later tokens already have the correct prefix in front of them (for example the first part of the name), so they are easier to guess by construction; this is expected and does not by itself mean the model knows surnames better.

§7结论 Conclusions

Qwen3-32B 对具体论文作者的知识在网络中形成得很晚，而且是逐层积累的：目标−对照的标题特异差从 L50 前后开始出现，在 L57–L61 上升最快，在 L62 和输出处最强。Qwen3-32B's knowledge of a specific paper's authors forms late and gradually: the target − control title-specific difference appears around L50, rises fastest over L57–L61, and is strongest at L62 and the output.
干预实验（Stage 11）显示，论文信息在 L24–L50 就被从标题读取并搬到标题之后的几个 token 上，L52 之后不再需要标题；只是这种形式要到 L56–62 才能在名字位置被 lens 读出。Interventions (Stage 11) show that the paper information is read from the title and moved into the tokens after it in L24–L50, and the title is no longer needed after L52; in that form it only becomes lens-readable at the name positions in L56–62.
"写不出但认得出"的目标组，并没有在中间层提前拿着作者信息；它们相对对照组的优势只出现在最后两层。Target papers (cannot write but recognise) do not hold the authors earlier in the middle layers; their advantage over control appears only in the last two layers.
这一优势几乎全部是标题特异的，在去掉低引用档后依然成立，不能用名字好猜或引用数差异来解释。That advantage is almost entirely title-specific and survives dropping the low-citation bins, so it is not explained by guessable names or citation differences.
Jacobian lens 能让中间层读出更多与名字相关的信息，但这部分信息不区分论文；三种 Jacobian lens 的结论一致。Jacobian lenses read more name-related information from middle layers than the logit lens, but that information is not paper-specific; all three Jacobian lenses agree.
标题特异信号在名字里的分布取决于格式：首字母几乎不带信号，信号集中在后面的姓上；完整的名本身就带有和姓相当的信号。知道第 1 个 token 后，后续 token 总体只容易一点（+0.04）；但完整的名进前 10 时，姓明显更容易（+0.22），首字母则没有这种帮助。Where the title-specific signal sits within a name depends on the format: an initial carries almost none and the signal concentrates on the surname after it, while a full given name carries about as much as the surname. Knowing the first token makes later tokens only slightly easier overall (+0.04), but a full given name in the top 10 makes the surname clearly easier (+0.22), while an initial does not.
引用越低，模型越不认识论文：标题特异信号随引用数单调上升（目标组从 +0.35 到 +0.64），在两组内部都成立。The fewer the citations, the less the model knows the paper: the title-specific signal rises monotonically with citations (target group from +0.35 to +0.64), within both groups.
没有哪种 lens 能在中间层读出偏向目标组的论文信息；Jacobian lens 在中间层多出的那点标题特异信号很小，对照组反而略多。No lens reads target-favouring paper information in middle layers; the small extra title-specific signal of the Jacobian lenses there is, if anything, larger for control papers.

§8局限 Limitations

  • 一致性检查的容差在看到结果后从 0.25 放宽到 0.5。差异的形态符合 bf16 舍入，但这是事后调整，且该检查无法发现两条计算路径共有的错误。The parity tolerance was raised from 0.25 to 0.5 after seeing the results. The differences look like bf16 rounding, but the change is post hoc, and the check cannot catch an error shared by both computation paths.
  • 分组来自同一模型的标注实验，只对 Qwen3-32B 有效；低引用档里的"认出"接近随机偏好，所以加做了去掉低档的敏感性分析。Groups come from a labelling run of the same model and hold for Qwen3-32B only; in low-citation bins recognition is close to a fixed preference, hence the sensitivity analysis without those bins.
  • 高引用档几乎没有对照组论文（4097+ 档为 0），组间比较主要来自中低引用档。High-citation bins have almost no control papers (none in 4097+), so group contrasts come mostly from low and middle bins.
  • 每位作者单独打分（接在 "Authors:" 后），不涉及作者顺序和合作者关系。Each author is scored alone after "Authors:"; author order and co-authorship are not tested.
  • 名次基于 bf16 输出的 logits，并列时按有利方向计（只数严格更大的 logit）。Ranks use bf16 logits; ties are counted favourably (only strictly greater logits count).
  • 细层（L50–55、L57–61）只算了 public Jacobian lens 和 logit lens（self100 和 fitA 只拟合了 6–7 个层，要加细层需要重新拟合）；§6.7–6.9 的补充分析只用了主轮的 6 个层。The fine layers (L50–55, L57–61) were computed for the public Jacobian lens and the logit lens only (self100 and fitA were fitted at 6–7 layers, so fine layers would need new fits); the follow-up analyses in §6.7–6.9 use the 6 main-round layers.
  • Stage 11 的替换和阻断确定了标题信息被读取的时间（L24–L50）和路径（经过标题之后的 token），但还没有确定这些层内部具体由哪些注意力头或 MLP 完成Stage 11 patching and knockout fix when (L24–L50) and through which positions (the tokens after the title) the title is read, but not which attention heads or MLPs do it inside those layers
  • 6.7 中"第 1 个 token 是否进前 10"的分组是观察性的，两组作者在名字格式、长度等方面不同；按名字格式分开后结论才稳定。The top-10 split in 6.7 is observational; the two sets of authors differ in name format and length, and the conclusion is stable only after separating name formats.
