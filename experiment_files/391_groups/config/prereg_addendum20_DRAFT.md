# Addendum 20 — DRAFT, NOT LOCKED (2026-11-19): H2 test with fictitious titles

Status: draft for the user. Not locked, no sha256, and no forward pass of the main experiment has been run. After the user approves it, the text is frozen (sha256 + lock json) before any GPU job of the main experiment is submitted.

## 1. Question
H2 says: "the title state encodes *which paper* it is". The existing evidence is mixed: M2 and X1 are positive, M3 does not support H2, and X3 cannot be separated from title meaning, so H2 stands as "partly supported, not established". A false title of another real paper is far from the true title in meaning (median mpnet cosine 0.09–0.11). So the true − false title-specific signal may come from:
- (a) the identity of the real paper (H2), or
- (b) the title text being close in meaning to topic-typical authors ("title-text similarity").

A title for a paper that does not exist, but is close in meaning to the true one, separates (a) from (b). It carries the topic but no identity:
- if (b) is right, it should recover a large share of the true − false gap;
- if (a) is right, it should recover little.

## 2. Design
- **Papers:** the Stage 11 subset (361_stage11/config/sample.parquet: 593 target + 593 control, 60 per group in each of 10 citation bins; 53 in 513-1024). Papers without a valid fictitious title are excluded (§6), leaving **1,127 papers: 566 target, 561 control**.
- **Conditions (same prompt and year as Stage 10):**
  - `true`;
  - `fict`: the v3 fictitious title (391_groups/h2_fictitious/v3/selected.csv, one per paper);
  - `false_0..false_4`: the existing Stage 10 metadata-matched titles of real other papers.

  Prompt for every condition: `Who are the authors of the paper titled "{title}" ({year})?\nAuthors:` + one author name, teacher-forced (the Stage 10 protocol, unchanged). The year is the true paper's year, as for the false titles.
- **Fictitious titles** (frozen before lock):
  - generated by Qwen3-32B (thinking off, T 0.7, 8 candidates, ≤ 2 regeneration rounds);
  - filters: no exact or near match among the 9,108 cohort titles; Crossref top 3 (top 10 with threshold 0.4 for true titles of ≤ 5 words) below jaccard2 0.5; jaccard2 with the true title < 0.3; length within max(±30 %, ±3) tokens and not shorter by instruction; mpnet cosine with the true title in [0.30, min(0.85, paraphrase cosine − 0.1)); no two-way entailment (NLI); one-way entailment > 0.9 rejected when cosine ≥ 0.70; different first word;
  - the selected title is the passing candidate with the highest cosine.
  - Selected set (n = 1,127): median cosine with the true title 0.65, against false_0 0.09 and the X3 paraphrase 0.97; median jaccard2 0.12; median length difference 0 %.
- **Readouts (Stage 10 metric unchanged):**
  - per name token, log10(1/rank) of the gold token among all vocabulary logits (bf16; rank = 1 + number of strictly larger logits);
  - per paper, mean over all scored name tokens of all authors (mean_nlr; the same token set in every condition);
  - readouts `final` (L63; primary) and, secondary, `logit` and `jlens_public` at the Stage 10 layers L24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 62.
- **Groups and weighting:**
  - target and control as in Stage 10/11 (`group_tight`);
  - target − control only within the 10 Stage 11 bins, with weights n_t·n_c/(n_t + n_c) on the remaining papers.
- **CIs:** author-team cluster bootstrap, 2,000 draws (team ids from the archived folds_own9108_k5s13.npz, `groups_core.Engine`, seed 20261120), percentile 95 %. Ratios are recomputed on each draw (ratio of means, not mean of ratios).

## 3. Primary comparison and decision rule
For paper i:
- S_c = mean_nlr at `final` L63 in condition c;
- F̄ = mean of S_false_0..4;
- G = S_true − F̄ (title-specific gap, Stage 10);
- A = S_fict − F̄ (what the fictitious title recovers);
- R = S_true − S_fict (what only the real title gives).

**Primary quantity, target group:**
- φ_T = Σ A / Σ G, the fictitious share of the true − false gap;
- with R̄_T = mean R.

**Decision rule (fixed before the run; four pre-registered readings, user 2026-11-19):**
The verdict uses the point estimate φ̂_T, read only when R̄_T's 95 % CI lies entirely above 0; the 95 % CI of φ_T is always reported beside it, and a verdict whose φ_T CI crosses one of the band limits (0.3 or 0.5) is labelled "borderline" (the band of the point estimate still decides the verdict).

| Result (target group) | Verdict |
|---|---|
| R̄_T 95 % CI entirely above 0 and φ̂_T < 0.3 | **H2 strongly supported**: a close, plausible but non-existent title recovers less than 30 % of the title-specific gap; most of the signal needs the real paper. |
| R̄_T 95 % CI entirely above 0 and 0.3 ≤ φ̂_T < 0.5 | **H2 partly supported** (limited weight: φ is near the value that meaning similarity alone predicts, ≈ 0.6). |
| R̄_T 95 % CI entirely above 0 and φ̂_T ≥ 0.5 | **H2 partly supported**: a real-paper component exists, but meaning similarity explains most of the signal. |
| R̄_T 95 % CI includes 0 | **H2 not supported**: the fictitious title gives as much as the true title, so the title-specific signal is explained by title-text similarity. |
| R̄_T 95 % CI entirely below 0 | **H2 refuted by a reversed result**: the non-existent title gives a larger title-specific signal than the real one. |

Why 0.5 and 0.3:
- The X3 meaning-preserving paraphrase (cosine ≈ 0.95–0.97) recovers about 0.92 of the original − partner gap (summary_x3/x3_behaviour.csv: original − partner 0.391, original − paraphrase 0.031). The partner there is not false_0, so this is an approximate benchmark.
- The fictitious titles sit at cosine ≈ 0.65, between the false titles (≈ 0.10) and the paraphrase (≈ 0.95).
- A pure title-text account therefore predicts φ roughly (0.65 − 0.10)/(0.95 − 0.10) × 0.92 ≈ 0.6.
- 0.5 sits below that prediction, and 0.3 well below it (about half of it); readings between 0.3 and 0.5 are therefore near the semantic prediction and carry limited weight. Both limits were chosen knowing the X3 result (§8); the four readings were fixed by the user on 2026-11-19 before any run.

## 4. Secondary comparisons (reported with CIs; no verdict of their own)
1. **Control group:** φ_C and R̄_C.
2. **Recognition and identity:** within-bin target − control of R and of φ. H2 predicts a larger real-paper component for recognised papers (R_T − R_C > 0).
3. **Layer profile at the name positions:** R_l and φ_l for `logit` and `jlens_public` at L24–L62. Descriptive: where the real-paper component becomes readable.
4. **E1 tail patching with the fictitious title as donor:**
   - in the false_0 run, the aligned post-title tail is replaced by the fict run's tail at the Stage 11 patch layers (L24, 32, 40, 48, 50, 52, 54, 56, 57–63);
   - rec_fict(l) = mean(patched − false_0) / mean(true − false_0), reported next to the published rec_true(l);
   - H2 predicts rec_fict(l ≥ 52) close to φ and well below rec_true (= 1.00 from L52);
   - L63 sanity check: rec_fict(63) = (fict − false_0)/(true − false_0) exactly.
5. **Title-position probe (E4b):** the probe trained on true runs (Stage 11 amendment 1, unchanged) applied to the fict run's last-title-token residual, at L16–L60. True − fict difference of the probe score for the gold author first token.
6. **M2 attribute readouts:**
   - the published M2 ridge probes (title-position residual → year, log citations, field; team folds) are applied to the fict residual and scored against the true paper's labels;
   - R² or accuracy is reported for true vs fict.
   - Title-text similarity predicts small differences for field and year. H2 predicts a larger drop for citations, the most identity-specific attribute. Descriptive.
7. **Meaning dose:** φ_T within tertiles of the fict cosine with the true title. Title-text similarity predicts φ rising with cosine; H2 predicts a low, flat φ.

## 5. Sensitivity (the primary verdict is restated only if a sensitivity analysis changes it)
- (a) false_0 instead of the 5-title mean.
- (b) Without papers whose fict title has Crossref jaccard2 ≥ 0.4 to any hit.
- (c) True titles with ≥ 6 words only (exclusions depend on title length, §6).
- (d) First author only; surname first token only.
- (e) R0 papers only.
- (f) **Title likelihood:** the fictitious titles were written by the same model and may be more "typical" to it. The mean per-token log-probability of the title tokens (prompt prefix only, from the same forward pass) is reported for true, fict and false. φ_T is also reported within strata of the true − fict title log-probability difference (tertiles).

## 6. Exclusions
- **Fixed by the generation rule:** 59 of 1,186 papers have no valid fictitious title after 2 regeneration rounds: 27 target, 32 control; at most 7 of 60 in any bin × group (0-2 control). Their true titles are short (median 5 words vs 12). List: h2_fictitious/v3/excluded.csv; table: exclusions_by_bin_group.csv.
- **Added at run time, with reasons logged:** prompts that exceed the Stage 10 maximum context; papers with a scoring failure. Any paper that also fails a hard check is reported, never silently dropped.
- Within-bin weights use the remaining papers.
